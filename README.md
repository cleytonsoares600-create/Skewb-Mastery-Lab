# 🧊 SkewbSphere — Algorithm Atlas & Spatial Trainer

> *A cube is not solved by hands alone — it is solved by the geometry your mind builds before your fingers ever move.*

Welcome to **SkewbSphere**, a next-generation companion workspace for twisty puzzle enthusiasts who want to map the Skewb's octahedral soul in three dimensions. Where other trainers hand you a list of moves, SkewbSphere hands you a *terrain*. Every scramble becomes a landscape. Every algorithm becomes a trail marker. Every first-layer solve becomes a summit you can revisit, annotate, and teach to someone else.

This project was born from a simple frustration: most Skewb resources treat the puzzle like a memory test. SkewbSphere treats it like a city you're learning to navigate — with districts, landmarks, shortcuts, and a few beautiful dead ends that teach you more than any perfect route ever could.

## 🌌 What Is SkewbSphere?

SkewbSphere is a practice and learning environment for the Skewb — that wonderfully disorienting corner-turning puzzle that refuses to behave like a normal cube. Instead of a flat sequence drill, SkewbSphere presents algorithms as *spatial relationships*. You rotate a model, highlight a corner, and watch the sequence ripple outward like rings on water.

The core philosophy: **understand the symmetry, and the moves will follow.** Our atlas is organized around the puzzle's rotational families, so once you recognize a pattern in one orientation, you recognize it in all eight.

Whether you're chasing faster first-layer solutions or exploring advanced last-layer permutations, SkewbSphere is the quiet studio where your spatial intuition sharpens.

## 🧠 The Algorithm Atlas

The Atlas is the heart of SkewbSphere. It is not a list. It is a *map*.

Each algorithm entry includes:

1. **Family badge** — which rotational group it belongs to
2. **Trigger rhythm** — the natural pulse of the sequence
3. **Mirror & inverse** — sibling algorithms presented side by side
4. **Common pitfalls** — the places where spatial reasoning most often slips
5. **Annotation slot** — your own mnemonic, in your own words

We believe a well-organized atlas should feel like a field guide to a forest — dense with detail, but never overwhelming, because the structure itself carries you forward.

## 🏗️ First Layer Foundations

The first layer is where beginners either fall in love with the Skewb or quietly give up. SkewbSphere refuses to let that second thing happen.

Our First Layer Workshop breaks the layer into *stations*, not steps:

- **Corner orientation stations** — build the visual vocabulary
- **Piece placement stations** — learn how each move echoes
- **Lock-in stations** — commit solutions to muscle and memory

Each station includes a practice loop, a reflection prompt, and a gentle nudge toward the next. No rush. No leaderboard pressure. Just steady progress.

## 🎨 Interface & Experience

The interface was designed by people who have spent too many hours staring at a puzzle and too few hours staring at ugly software.

- Soft gradients instead of harsh grids
- Animation that supports comprehension rather than decorating it
- Keyboard, mouse, and touch parity
- A status ribbon that tells you exactly what the puzzle is doing

We treat the UI like the margin of a well-loved notebook — present, useful, but never louder than the thing you're actually studying.
